2  REQUIREMENTS ANALYSIS

2.1  Functional Requirements

\begin{tabular}{|p{2.5cm}|p{9.5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Module} & \textbf{Requirement} \\ \hline
Authentication & Login, logout, password reset, role-based access \\ \hline
Dashboard & Role-specific views for each user type \\ \hline
Student Mgmt & Add, edit, delete, search, promote students \\ \hline
Teacher Mgmt & Add, update, delete teacher profiles \\ \hline
Class \& Subject & Create classes, sections, assign subjects \\ \hline
Timetable & Weekly timetable creation and management \\ \hline
Attendance & Mark daily attendance, generate reports \\ \hline
Examination & Schedule exams, enter marks, publish results \\ \hline
Assignments & Create, submit, track homework \\ \hline
Fees & Fee structures, payments, receipts \\ \hline
Transport & Bus routes, vehicles, student mapping \\ \hline
Notice Board & Announcements, events, holiday lists \\ \hline
Reports & Admission, attendance, exam reports \\ \hline
\end{tabular}

2.2  Non-Functional Requirements

Security with JWT authentication, performance within 3-second page loads, scalability, responsive usability, and reliable error handling.

2.3  User Roles

\textbf{Administrator:} Full access to all modules and system settings. \textbf{Principal:} View all academic and administrative data, approve requests. \textbf{Teacher:} Manage classes, mark attendance, create assignments, enter marks. \textbf{Student:} View attendance, timetable, results, assignments, fee status. \textbf{Parent:} Monitor child's attendance, performance, homework, and fee dues.

2.4  Modules

SchoolHub includes 13 modules: Authentication, Dashboard, Student Management, Teacher Management, Academic Management, Timetable, Attendance, Examination, Assignments, Fees, Transport, Notice Board, and Reports.
