\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}

\section{Conclusion}

SchoolHub is a web-based School Management System built with React, TypeScript, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, and Prisma ORM. It provides role-based access for Administrators, Teachers, Students, and Parents with modules for student management, attendance, examinations, assignments, fees, and communication. The project demonstrates a complete DevOps workflow using GitHub, Docker, Jenkins, and Azure DevOps for CI/CD deployment.

\section{Future Enhancements}

AI Chatbot, QR and Face Recognition Attendance, Online Fee Payment, Mobile App, Email and SMS Notifications, and Analytics Dashboard.
